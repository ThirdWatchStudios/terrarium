# QuotaCo accepted open-pocket T-junction proof family

This external proof bank contains one fixed-light source pair for the first
accepted ordinary three-way wall junction:

- `open_w_t_junction-*` is the authored direct source for `mask_7`: north, east,
  and south are connected; west is exposed; northeast and southeast remain
  open pockets.
- `mask_13` is the approved whole-cell X-mirror derivation after omitting the
  source-owned east boundary seam under the accepted southeast seam filter.
- The vertical spine remains continuous through the hub. The east branch grows
  from that spine as one molded catalog part rather than as a corner pasted on
  top of a straight wall.
- At the crook, the branch-owned cream coping crosses the compressed vertical
  coral/green stack and reaches the adjoining horizontal cell. The coral turns
  underneath it; neither material may stop short at the internal join.
- The cream plane, coral register, green handoff, plinth, and service seams
  retain the accepted fixed-light ownership of their outgoing wall sockets.
- No cap, central post, four-way hub, door state, or filled diagonal is part of
  this family.

The accepted proof ledger records `mask_7` as direct and `mask_13` as an
approved filtered X-mirror derivation, bringing its totals to 11 direct, 10
derived, 26 synthetic, and 0 unresolved. This acceptance is proof-layer
provenance only: it does not register canonical or production stems, frame
identities, templates, exporter routes, atlas frames, schema values, blob
mappings, or Unity assets.
