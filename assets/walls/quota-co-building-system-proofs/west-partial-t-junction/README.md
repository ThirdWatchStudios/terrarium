# QuotaCo accepted west-side partial T-junction proof sources

This external proof bank contains the two separately authored fixed-light source
pairs accepted as direct proof-layer mappings for the single-filled-pocket
states between the open-west T-junction and the filled west-side repeat:

- `open_w_t_filled_ne-*` directly authors `mask_17`: north, east, and
  south remain connected; the northeast diagonal is occupied; the southeast
  crook remains open floor.
- `open_w_t_filled_se-*` directly authors `mask_21`: north, east, and
  south remain connected; the southeast diagonal is occupied; the northeast
  crook remains open floor.
- Together they complete the two sibling branches of the occupancy diamond from
  open `mask_7`, through one filled crook (`mask_17` or `mask_21`), to fully
  filled `mask_24`. They are not a progression from `mask_17` to `mask_21`.

Both pairs retain the accepted 128 by 128 footprint, separate `detail/base`
and `detail/upper` groups, the west-side fixed-light register, and exact
north/east/south continuation sockets. The occupied half is authored as solid
cream wall top at source; its buried coral, green, charcoal-face, and service
seam geometry is absent rather than covered by a decorative patch. The open
half preserves the molded branch turn and one readable concave floor pocket.

The northeast-filled `mask_17` source keeps the accepted south-facing material
shade, clips its contact shadow around the continuing south socket, and matches
the foreground `mask_38` east socket. The southeast-filled
`mask_21` source uses the accepted cream-only `mask_31` rear socket and rear
solid-top highlight instead; it is separately authored and is not a Y mirror.

The east-side counterparts are accepted approved derivations of this source
bank. `mask_36` is the whole-cell X mirror of `mask_17` after the accepted
shared-boundary seam filter, including the clipped south-socket contact-shadow
return; `mask_27` is the plain whole-cell X mirror of `mask_21`. No separate
east SVG source bank is introduced.

Acceptance records direct proof-layer provenance only. These files do not
register canonical or production stems, change blob connectivity, add exporter
or atlas routes, change schema, or authorize Unity assets.
