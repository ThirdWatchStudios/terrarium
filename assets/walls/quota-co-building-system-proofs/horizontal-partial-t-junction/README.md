# QuotaCo accepted horizontal partial T-junction proof sources

This external proof bank contains two fixed-light source pairs for the remaining
horizontal-spine one-filled-pocket T-junction states:

- `open_s_t_filled_ne-*` directly authors foreground
  `mask_18`: north, east, and west stay connected; the northeast diagonal is
  solid; the northwest crook remains open floor.
- `open_n_t_filled_se-*` directly authors rear `mask_22`:
  east, south, and west stay connected; the southeast diagonal is solid; the
  southwest crook remains open floor.
- `mask_35` is the accepted whole-cell X mirror of `mask_18` after omitting
  `base-boundary-seam` and `upper-boundary-seam`.
- `mask_28` is the accepted plain whole-cell X mirror of `mask_22`.

The sources are fixed-view structural unions, not an open T with a patch laid
over one crook. Buried fascia, branch material, and service ticks are absent at
source. The direct foreground master owns its east boundary seam; the filtered
mirror leaves that shared seam to its accepted west neighbor. The rear master
is seam-free and cream-led on its filled side.

The current source geometry is owner accepted unchanged. Cross-family
south-face plane-cue continuity is deliberately deferred until the complete
wall family can be polished under one exposure and clipping rule; this
acceptance does not claim a continuous shade across adjoining pieces.

Acceptance records proof-layer source provenance for `mask_18`, `mask_35`,
`mask_22`, and `mask_28` only. These files do not register canonical or
production stems, alter blob connectivity, add exporter or atlas routes,
change schema, or authorize Unity assets.
