# QuotaCo 2xN thick-wall repeat proof

This external source bank is the owner-accepted proof-layer source for the
repeatable middle row of a solid, two-cell-thick wall mass.

- `filled_w_middle-*` authors `mask_24` in fixed-light west-side coordinates.
- `mask_42` is the accepted whole-cell X mirror of that source.
- The accepted matrix is `20 26 / 24 42 / 16 34`; the corner rows remain
  controls and the middle pair may repeat to form longer 2×N masses.
- The source continues one cream wall-top plane through both Y sockets. It owns
  no cap, rollover, coral/green fascia, south-facing shade, or internal seam.
- These files do not register production stems, exporter routes, atlas frames,
  schema values, blob mappings, or Unity assets.

At this family's acceptance checkpoint the proof-layer ledger recorded 8 direct,
9 derived, 30 synthetic, and 0 unresolved rows; later source-family decisions
supersede those counts. Acceptance records source provenance only; the production
registry, exporter, atlas, schema, and Unity remain unchanged.
