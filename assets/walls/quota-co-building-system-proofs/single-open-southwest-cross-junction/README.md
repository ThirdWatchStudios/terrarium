# Single-open cross-junction accepted proof source

Owner-accepted proof-layer source bank for equal-height wall `mask_41` and its
approved whole-cell X-mirror companion `mask_44`.

The accepted source connects all four cardinal sockets, fills the northeast,
southeast, and northwest diagonals, and leaves the southwest crook open to
floor. The accepted north-filled `mask_39` and east-filled `mask_25` sources
constrain its topology, while accepted `mask_40` is the explicit
southwest-return material/register control. All three remain cues only: this
source is one independently authored, flattened union rather than stacked
source art.

For `mask_44`, the same complete pair is reused through one plain whole-cell X
mirror around `x=64`. That derivation fills northwest, northeast, and southwest
while leaving the southeast crook open to floor. No seam path is filtered, no
companion SVG is authored, and the source's Y-based cream, coral, green,
face-shade, and contact-shadow hierarchy remains intact.

The two SVGs preserve the normal proof-layer split:

- `open_cross_filled_ne_se_nw-base.svg` owns one lower structural mass and the
  exposed material depth around the southwest return.
- `open_cross_filled_ne_se_nw-upper.svg` owns the sole cream shell and the
  coral/green register visible at that one open crook. Its shade, coral, green,
  highlight, and seam follow the same curved southwest return into the south
  socket instead of ending on a square interior shelf.

This directory records the accepted direct proof-layer source for ledger row
`mask_41` and the approved plain-X derivation for ledger row `mask_44`. It does
not register a production sprite, change exporter or atlas behavior, modify the
canonical blob contract or schema, or authorize Unity consumption.
