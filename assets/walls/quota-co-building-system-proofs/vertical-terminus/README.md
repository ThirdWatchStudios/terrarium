# Accepted equal-height vertical terminus proof sources

These four SVGs are an isolated, owner-accepted proof-layer source bank for the two
vertical one-link cases in the equal-height wall family:

- `vertical_s_terminus-*` — connected north, exposed end south (`mask_1`)
- `vertical_n_terminus-*` — connected south, exposed end north (`mask_4`)

The two directions are authored independently for the fixed high-oblique view.
They must not be rotated or Y-mirrored into one another. The workbench may show
each west-authored pair through a whole-cell X mirror for the matching east-wall
presentation; those mirrors are accepted proof-ledger derivations.

The wall itself must terminate; these sources do not add a second catalog item
to it:

- both ends stay inside the ordinary west-wall cross-section with no shoulder,
  sleeve, badge, service plate, or local widening;
- the south-facing end exposes more of the foreground rollover, while the north
  end exposes more of the broad cream top plane; they are separately authored,
  not Y-mirrored;
- the existing cream, coral, and green wall registers remain continuous and
  bend through each shallow molded closure;
- at the north end, coral and green stay longitudinal into the rounded envelope;
  neither may transpose into a full-width bar across the cream plane;
- the south source preserves the accepted body through source row 79 and the
  north source returns to the exact body by source row 45;
- the unlabeled 40 px cells and two-cell end-to-end segment are decisive: the
  result must read as one constant-width tri-tone wall, never two fittings joined
  by a pipe.

This directory sits outside the canonical Building System B importer. The proof
ledger records these filenames as accepted provenance for `mask_1` and `mask_4`,
but nothing here is registered, exported, added to a production atlas, or
consumed by Unity.
