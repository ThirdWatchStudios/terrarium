cell of a solid wall mass:

- north, east, south, and west are connected;
- northeast, southeast, southwest, and northwest are all solid;
- no floor crook or exterior wall face is visible.

The accepted perimeter cells own every visible outline, coping highlight,
coral register, green frontage, south-facing material shade, and floor-contact
plinth. The accepted `filled_center` pair therefore owns only:

- one full-cell charcoal structural underlay; and

shadow, cap, or return. Stacking the accepted horizontal and vertical middle
sources would leave a false internal charcoal seam, so they are geometry and
registration controls only, not source provenance.
