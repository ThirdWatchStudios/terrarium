# Double-filled south cross-junction proof source

Owner-accepted proof-layer source bank for equal-height wall `mask_32`.

The accepted source joins all four cardinal sockets, keeps the northeast and
northwest crooks open to floor, and closes the southeast and southwest
diagonals into one continuous south slab. It is authored directly in the
fixed high-oblique view; the accepted north-filled `mask_39` source is a
topology control, not a source to flip on Y.

The two SVGs are deliberately split into the normal proof-only base and upper
layers:

- `open_cross_filled_s-base.svg` owns the buried south structure and the
  visible side register of the north spur.
- `open_cross_filled_s-upper.svg` owns one continuous cream shell. Coral and
  green remain vertical on the north spur; no buried horizontal fascia crosses
  the south slab.

This directory records the accepted direct proof-layer source for `mask_32`.
It does not register a production sprite, alter exporter or atlas behavior,
change the canonical blob contract, or authorize Unity consumption.
