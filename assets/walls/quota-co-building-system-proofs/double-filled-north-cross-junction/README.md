# QuotaCo double-filled north cross-junction proof source

This directory contains the owner-accepted authored proof source pair for
`mask_39`. The ledger maps that row as a direct reuse at the proof layer; the
files are still not canonical production, exporter, atlas, schema, blob, or
Unity-registration art.

## Topology

- cardinal sockets: north, east, south, west
- solid diagonals: northeast, northwest
- open floor crooks: southeast, southwest
- canonical blob value: `0x9f`

The two northern filled cells read as one uninterrupted molded cream slab.
At the south socket, that cream top continues directly into the branch top:
the horizontal frontage shade, coral belt, and green handoff stop at the
socket instead of painting across it. Their exposed left and right runs turn
down the west-fixed south branch while both southern crooks remain open floor.

## Source ownership

- `open_cross_filled_n-base.svg` owns the flattened lower structure and a
  buried north underlay. It does not repaint a buried north-facing fascia.
- `open_cross_filled_n-upper.svg` owns the sole cream shell, its continuous
  top-to-top south-socket join, the exposed south-facing depth treatment, and
  both open southern returns.

These files are one directly authored fixed-view union. They must not be
assembled by stacking the accepted northeast- and northwest-filled sources,
and must not introduce a cap, peak, post, patch, rollover, transform, or
duplicate cream owner.

Owner review accepted the composed compact and extended masses. Ledger row
`39` is now an `accepted-source-mapping / direct-reuse`; this promotion does
not cross the separate production-art boundary.
