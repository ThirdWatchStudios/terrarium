# QuotaCo accepted horizontal-spine open-pocket T-junction proof family

This external proof bank holds the two owner-accepted fixed-light direct sources
for the ordinary equal-height wall family.

- `open_s_t_junction-*` is the direct source for `mask_11`: north, east,
  and west connected; south exposed; northeast and northwest pockets open.
- `open_n_t_junction-*` is the separately authored direct source for
  `mask_14`: east, south, and west connected; north exposed; southeast and
  southwest pockets open.

The sources are deliberately not Y mirrors. Each must choose one high-oblique
perspective, carry its cream/coral/green stack through the bend, and read as a
single QuotaCo catalog junction rather than a cap, post, patch, or filled
four-way hub.

Both candidates must survive:

- 240, 90, and 40 pixel-per-cell reads;
- one-cell compact arms and six-cell horizontal/vertical extents;
- light and dark floors; and
- both accepted vertical-branch facings, so a hidden `mask_5` socket-position
  collision cannot be mistaken for a valid universal junction.

This is a proof bank, not a production source directory. `mask_11` and
`mask_14` are accepted `direct-reuse / accepted-source-mapping` ledger rows,
bringing the proof ledger to 13 direct, 10 derived, 24 synthetic, and 0 unresolved.
The lateral X-mirror presentations remain comparison evidence only; no mirror
derivation is accepted for either source. Acceptance does not register canonical
authored stems, alter the 47-mask blob contract, change the exporter or schema,
commit atlas frames, or authorize Unity integration.
